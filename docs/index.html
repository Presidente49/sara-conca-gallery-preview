The artist

Sara Conca

American contemporary abstract artist.
Working with light, movement and natural materials.

“My work is intended to instantaneously immerse the viewer into the visual and emotional energies that I have devoted to each piece.”
Sara Conca · Artist statement

Behind the surface

Conca paints in reverse on clear acrylic, beginning with the marks nearest the viewer and building back through layers of color.

Silver dust, gold leaf, mica, pigments and natural crystals create surfaces that respond to their surroundings. The appearance shifts with the light and the position of the viewer.
